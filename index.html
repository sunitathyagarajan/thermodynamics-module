Principles of Thermodynamics

Why does a hot cup of coffee cool down? Where does the energy from burning fuel go? How much work can an expanding gas actually do? Thermodynamics answers these questions with a few powerful ideas — and this module walks you through them with diagrams, animations and practice problems.

Colour key used throughout: system surroundings heat, q work, w internal energy, U

By the end of this module you should be able to…

  • Explain what thermodynamics studies, distinguish state functions from path functions, and explain why U is a state function while q and w are path functions.
  • Identify the system and surroundings and classify systems as open, closed or isolated.
  • Describe internal energy (U), heat (q) and work (w) and apply the correct sign conventions.
  • State the first law (ΔU = q + w) and use it for gases, liquids, solids and chemical reactions.
  • Calculate pressure–volume work for expansions and compressions.
  • Distinguish reversible from irreversible heat and work, compare them quantitatively, and explain why the difference is tiny for solids and liquids.
